## What I build

- **AI agents.** Planning and content agents with structured (Zod-validated) outputs, persistent memory, human-in-the-loop approvals, and a full audit trail.
- **Copilot Studio and Microsoft 365 Copilot agents.** Proposal and SOW generation, project status reporting, and customer knowledge bases built from meeting transcripts and SharePoint documents.
- **IDP and AP automation.** Invoice extraction, new-vendor learning, approval routing, and automated line-item and three-way matching with ERP integration.
- **Multi-LLM apps.** One app layer over OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI (Grok), and Google Gemini, with bring-your-own-key settings, encrypted key storage, and per-call token and cost tracking.
- **Enterprise integration.** API and SQL integrations across ECM, ERP, and CRM platforms (Oracle, NetSuite, Dynamics, Infor, Sage, Salesforce, SharePoint, Epic).

## How I work

- **Start with the process, not the model.** I begin with use-case discovery, workflow design, and output validation, and only then pick a model.
- **Keep humans in the loop.** Agents propose, people approve, and every decision is logged.
- **Measure adoption.** Pilots are evaluated with the people who use them, and ROI is tracked from the start.
